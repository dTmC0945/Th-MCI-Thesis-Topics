1.3 Fault Detection in Electrical Machines ¶

Electrical machines, while the backbone of industry, are prone to various faults and require constant monitoring to ensure safe and dependable functioning.

A potential fault in electrical machinery results in unscheduled downtime, necessitating the prompt assessment of any abnormal circumstances in rotating electrical machines.

The work will require to implement machine learning algorithms for the service of fault detection.

Previous Knowledge

The student is required to have a working knowledge of:

  • A good understanding of machine learning/signal processing is preferred,
  • A strong background in programming,
  • Working knowledge of electrodynamics is beneficial.

Outcome

The outcome will be a work producing a possible novel approach to detecting electrical faults in an electrical motor.

The student will gain significant experience working with signal processing and programming and developing large machine learning algorithms.
