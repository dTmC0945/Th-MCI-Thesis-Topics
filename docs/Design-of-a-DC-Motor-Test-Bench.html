2.3 Design of a DC Motor Test Bench ¶

The students should have knowledge of:

  • Circuit design,
  • CAD software experience,
  • Basic knowledge on electric machines is a plus.

DC Drives are one of the more versatile machines still used in industry due to their cost effective design and simple control methodologies. One of the advantages is their ability to adapt based on how their wiring is done for field and armature. This work will require the construction of a DC motor test bench for use in electric machine lectures.

The outcome will be a working platform where the machine dynamic can be changed based on how the connections are done. These connections will be designed in a way as to easily be changed by the user. The student shall also gain practical experience with electric drive design and work with manufacturing of a product which takes different parts of electrical engineering and combine them. The student will also contribute to the development of new hardware to be used by the next generation of students.
